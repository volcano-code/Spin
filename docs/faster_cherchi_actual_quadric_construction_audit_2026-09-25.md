## Limits and next gate

This proves static combinatorial/source identity for these fixed synthetic
triangulations only. It does not derive the movement of LPI/TPI/LNC points
under coefficient changes, prove differentiability across topology events,
or resolve the already measured exact-solid discrepancies between Cherchi
and CGAL for several quadric types. The solver must not consume this as a
Newton Jacobian. The next integration gate is a motion derivative from these
retained construction relations, checked against the actual Cherchi
Boolean-to-moment map on stable-topology coefficient perturbations; any
ambiguous source relation or topology change must fail closed.
