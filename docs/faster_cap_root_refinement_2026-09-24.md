## Confirmed defect and repair

The hyperboloid triangulator previously stopped after 80 bisections in normal
space and emitted the last inside inverse-Gauss point. That point satisfied
the quadric equation but could miss the truncation sphere by roughly
`9e-10`–`1.3e-8`. Source-motion reconstruction has a stricter, intentional
cap-radius condition (`1e-10 * max(1,R)`), so valid solver trials were rejected
before the differential could be formed.

Each crossing is now locally refined in position against the same three
geometric constraints:

1. `q(x) = 0` (quadric surface),
2. `|x|^2 = R^2` (truncation sphere),
3. `n_edge · grad(q(x)) = 0` (normal lies in the crossed icosphere-edge plane).

The bounded Newton/line-search refinement either meets the strict residual
contract or fails explicitly; it does not loosen the source-motion tolerance.
The captured unknown-seed trial is a regression in both triangulation and
source-motion tests.
